Privacy

Scribecue is an app for writing audio description. This page says what it does with your information.

Your work stays with you

  • Projects, videos, recordings and settings stay on your computer, in your browser and in files you save.
  • Scribecue has no accounts, and keeps no copy of your work.
  • There are no ads and no tracking.

Services you choose

Some features use an online service, only once you set them up with your own key:

  • AI help: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini or OpenRouter.
  • Voices: Google Cloud text to speech or Amazon Polly.
  • Subtitles and film details: OpenSubtitles and TMDB.

Your browser sends what each feature needs straight to that service. Scribecue doesn't see it. Each service's own privacy policy applies.

Sharing a project

Live sharing goes through Scribecue's sharing service. Projects are encrypted on your computer first, so the service can't read them. It sees when people connect, and how big the changes are. A shared project nobody opens for 180 days is deleted from it.

Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. It keeps your settings the same in every browser you use, and lets you keep projects there to open on any computer.

  • What Scribecue can reach: a hidden folder in your Drive that's Scribecue's own, and the files Scribecue made itself. It can't see or change your other files.
  • What it keeps there: your Scribecue settings, in the hidden folder. The keys to shared projects you started or joined, so they open in any browser. Your API keys too, only if you turn on Sync API keys. Projects you save to Google Drive go in a Scribecue folder you can see. Videos and recordings stay on your computer.
  • Your email address is shown in Settings, so you know which account is connected. It isn't kept anywhere else.
  • Scribecue's sign-in service renews Google's permission while you stay connected. It keeps nothing: your browser holds the permission, sealed so it only works through that service.

Scribecue's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It's used only to sync your settings and projects for you. It's never sold, shared, used for ads, or read by people.

To disconnect, click Disconnect in Settings → Google Drive. You can also remove Scribecue at myaccount.google.com/connections.

To delete what's in Drive, delete the Scribecue folder for projects. For settings, open Google Drive, go to Settings → Manage apps, and choose Delete hidden app data next to Scribecue.
